# VWD clinical-agent pilot data

`vwd_agentic_workflow_deidentified_v3.xlsx` is the research-only, de-identified
copy of the physician-provided pilot workbook.

- Direct patient names were removed.
- Research IDs preserve patient/variant linkage without storing a name mapping.
- The workbook contains 47 unique cases and 59 case/variant rows.
- `Sheet1` contains the physician-corrected workflow dated 2026-08-25.
- The workbook does not contain a complete expert gold standard for subtype or
  next-action accuracy.

Do not commit the original workbook or any reversible identity mapping. This
dataset is for retrospective research and expert review, not autonomous
diagnosis or direct patient management.
